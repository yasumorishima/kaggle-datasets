ABS Challenges: Triple-A 2025 to MLB 2026 - File Descriptions

Use the text below for each file's "About this file" section on Kaggle.

----------------------------------------------------------------
abs_challenges_players.csv
----------------------------------------------------------------
Baseball Savant's ABS (Automated Ball-Strike) challenge leaderboard, 15 boards: MLB 2025 spring
training (the test), Triple-A 2025 and 2026 regular season, MLB 2026 spring training and regular season,
each for batters, pitchers and catchers as the challenger. One row per player per board, keyed
by the MLBAM player_id (Savant's own CSV export has no id). Boards were fetched with a minimum of
0 challenges, so players Savant counts pitches for but who never challenged are included.
Columns ending in _against describe challenges made against the player's side.
Collected with the savant-extras Python package (0.6.0). Added per player, for the board's own
level, season and game type: bio (bats, throws, age, height, weight, position, debut) and season
stats from MLB StatsAPI (api_bat_, api_pit_, api_c_), and Statcast search aggregates (sc_bat_,
sc_pit_, sc_c_: plate discipline, chase and zone swing rates, xwOBA, exit velocity, and for
catchers the called-strike rate on taken pitches outside the zone and called-ball rate inside it).


----------------------------------------------------------------
abs_aaa2025_to_mlb2026.csv
----------------------------------------------------------------
Players on both the Triple-A 2025 and the MLB 2026 regular-season boards (batters and catchers,
joined on player_id), with the same challenge measures side by side: columns ending in _aaa2025
come from Triple-A 2025, columns ending in _mlb2026 from MLB 2026. Only players with at least one
pitch in Savant's sample (n_total_sample > 0) on each side are included. Pitchers are left out:
Triple-A pitchers made 5 challenges in all of 2025. Bio columns appear once; season stats and
Statcast aggregates (api_bat_, api_c_, sc_bat_, sc_c_) appear for each side with the same suffix.
