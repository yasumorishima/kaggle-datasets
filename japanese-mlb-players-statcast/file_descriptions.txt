================================================================
Japanese MLB Players Statcast 2015-2026 - File Descriptions
================================================================
Use the text below for each file's "About this file" section on Kaggle.
================================================================


----------------------------------------------------------------
japanese_mlb_pitching.csv
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every regular-season pitch thrown in MLB from 2015 to 2026 by the pitchers in players.csv
(Japan-born players; Shohei Ohtani is in both pitch files). One row per pitch, exactly as
Baseball Savant's Statcast search serves it (all of Savant's columns, values unchanged), plus
player_name_eng (the full name from MLB StatsAPI). The pitcher column is always the Japanese
player; join it to players.csv or season_summary.csv on mlbam_id. Regular season only
(game_type R). Every player-season was checked against MLB StatsAPI: id, name, season, game type,
and a pitch count equal to StatsAPI's (Savant's automatic_ball / automatic_strike rows,
which are not pitches, excluded).


----------------------------------------------------------------
japanese_mlb_batting.csv
----------------------------------------------------------------
Every regular-season pitch seen in MLB from 2015 to 2026 by the batters in players.csv, including
the pitchers' own plate appearances and the two US-born players of
Japanese heritage (Lars Nootbaar, Gosuke Katoh). One row per pitch, exactly as Baseball Savant's
Statcast search serves it, plus player_name_eng. The batter column is always the player of the row;
join it to players.csv or season_summary.csv on mlbam_id. Regular season only (game_type R), checked
against MLB StatsAPI like the pitching file.


----------------------------------------------------------------
players.csv
----------------------------------------------------------------
One row per player: every player born in Japan (MLB StatsAPI birthCountry) with an MLB
regular-season game from 2015 to 2026, plus two US-born players of Japanese heritage kept from the
earlier version. MLBAM id, name, birth country, city and date, position, bats/throws, debut date,
MLB teams and the seasons with rows in each pitch file. in_previous_version and previous_wrong_id
record what changed from the earlier (February 2026) version, which had wrong ids for Hisashi
Iwakuma and Yuki Matsui. The earlier npb_team and note columns were hand-typed and are not included.


----------------------------------------------------------------
season_summary.csv
----------------------------------------------------------------
One row per player, season and role (pitcher or batter): MLB StatsAPI regular-season stats (games,
pitches, plate appearances or innings, rate stats) and MLB StatsAPI sabermetrics (wOBA, wRC+, FIP,
xFIP, WAR; MLB's own calculation, not FanGraphs'), next to statcast_rows, the number of rows of the
same player-season-role in the pitch file. Useful to filter the pitch files by workload or to join
season outcomes to pitch-level features.
